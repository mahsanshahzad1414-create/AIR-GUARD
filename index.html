How AIR-GUARD works

We combine modeled air-quality and weather signals, then translate them into a simple risk score and actionable guidance. This is an environmental awareness tool, not a medical diagnosis.

Data transparency

Air-quality and weather values are supplied by Open-Meteo environmental models. Local measurements can differ from modeled conditions.
