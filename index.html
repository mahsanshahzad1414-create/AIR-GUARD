PM2.5

PM2.5 refers to very small airborne particles that can penetrate deep into the respiratory system.

PM10

PM10 consists of inhalable particles such as dust, construction particles and other airborne material.

NO₂

Nitrogen dioxide is commonly associated with combustion processes, especially traffic and fuel burning.

O₃

Ground-level ozone is a secondary pollutant formed through chemical reactions involving sunlight and precursor pollutants.
